##  How Our Technology Works

### Real-Time Market Analysis

Our AI continuously evaluates:

-   Price movements
-   Market momentum
-   Trading volume
-   Volatility
-   Liquidity conditions
-   Broader market signals

This allows the system to identify developing market opportunities and
changing risk conditions.

### Intelligent Entry & Exit Decisions

The system executes trades only when defined market signals and risk
conditions align.

Each position follows structured rules based on:

-   Entry conditions
-   Exit strategies
-   Profit targets
-   Stop-loss protection
-   Market condition changes

### Automated Capital Protection

Risk management is built into every trading decision through:

-   Maximum position exposure controls
-   Leverage management
-   Daily loss limits
-   Portfolio drawdown protection
-   Dynamic risk adjustments

### Dynamic Position Management

The system adjusts position sizing based on:

-   Available capital
-   Market volatility
-   Signal strength
-   Liquidity conditions
-   Current portfolio exposure

This approach helps maintain disciplined execution across different
market environments.
